$\left(5+2\sqrt6\right)\left(\sqrt6-2\right)\sqrt{5-2\sqrt6}$ ifadəsinin qiymətini hesablayın.

A) $2\sqrt2$  B) $\sqrt2$  C) $1$  D) $2$  E) $\sqrt3$

**Düzgün cavab:** B

**Həll.** $5\pm2\sqrt6=\left(\sqrt3\pm\sqrt2\right)^2$, $\sqrt6-2=\sqrt2\left(\sqrt3-\sqrt2\right)$. İfadə: $\left(\sqrt3+\sqrt2\right)^2\cdot\sqrt2\left(\sqrt3-\sqrt2\right)\cdot\left(\sqrt3-\sqrt2\right)=\sqrt2\left(3-2\right)^2=\sqrt2$.

A) $3\sqrt2$  B) $\sqrt2$  C) $9\sqrt2$  D) $18$  E) $9$

**Düzgün cavab:** C

**Həll.** $7\pm2\sqrt{10}=\left(\sqrt5\pm\sqrt2\right)^2$, $\sqrt{10}-2=\sqrt2\left(\sqrt5-\sqrt2\right)$. İfadə: $\sqrt2\left(\left(\sqrt5+\sqrt2\right)\left(\sqrt5-\sqrt2\right)\right)^2=\sqrt2\cdot3^2=9\sqrt2$.
